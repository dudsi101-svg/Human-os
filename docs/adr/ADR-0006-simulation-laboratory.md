# ADR-0006: Simulation and Scenario Laboratory

## Status
Accepted

## Decision
Material actions may be tested in a sandbox before execution using explicit
initial conditions, interventions, uncertainty ranges, constitutional invariants,
counterfactual comparisons and pre-execution safety gates.

## Constitutional rationale
Simulation is advisory. It may reveal risk but cannot replace human judgment.

## Limitation
The current model uses simplified additive effects. Results are hypotheses, not
forecasts of real human behaviour.
